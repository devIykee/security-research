ABOUT THE COLLECTION

What are Arclings?

Each Arcling is a 40×40 monochrome bitmap — 1,600 pixels that form a unique, living arc character. Every Arcling is mathematically generated on-chain.

The artwork isn't hosted on servers or IPFS. It lives entirely inside Arc smart contracts, rendered as clean SVG vectors on demand.

Arclings exist in standard "Ink on Paper" and rare "Inverted" styles, carrying combinations of unique traits like antennas, haloes, pebbles, and different body fill patterns.

On-Chain Architecture

1,600
Bits per Face

A 40×40 monochrome grid where each bit represents a pixel. MSB-first binary encoding packs the visual asset extremely tightly.

100%
On-Chain SVGs

SVGs are generated dynamically on-chain using row-scan RLE compression. The code, traits, and metadata are permanent.

6,283
Limit Supply

The total supply matches the mathematical constant Tau (2π ≈ 6.283), marking the complete rotation of the circle.

Built To Be Used

Arclings operates the official collection, website, and mint. The artwork is released under CC0 so builders can remix, print, or integrate the pixel data. The monochrome 1-bit aesthetic is a design constraint: simple, lightweight, and composable across clients.

Roadmap

6,283 little arcs that came to life. 1-bit. Fully on-chain. Born on Arc. When the last one mints, the circle closes.

Arclings is the first fully on-chain identity collection on Arc — Circle's Layer-1. Each Arcling is a tiny living arc drawn in strict 1-bit (two colors, ink on paper), generated from its token ID and stored entirely inside the smart contract. No IPFS, no servers, no hosted images. As long as Arc exists, every Arcling exists.

The supply is not a marketing number. A full circle is 6.283 radians, and Arc is built by Circle — so there are exactly 6,283 Arclings. Most are open arcs; a rare few close into a full circle. The final mint, #6283, is the one that completes it.

PHASE 0 IN PROGRESS

Foundations & Build

Concept formulation, 1-bit art generation, and contract deployment.

  • Concept locked: 1-bit living-arc creatures and closed-circle rarity design.
  • Full art system: 8 trait layers, 6,283 pieces generated and verified unique.
  • ERC-721 smart contract: on-chain SVG renderer & math-constant grail logic.
  • Testnet validator: deployed and verified on Arc L1 testnet.
PHASE 1 LAUNCH

Mint & Launch

Community build-up, contract mainnet deploy, and the mint phase.

  • Warm up X account: release mathematical circle-closing teasers.
  • Deploy tested contract to Arc mainnet at launch.
  • Mint portal: connect wallet, live mint counter tracking progress.
  • Grail claims & closing: #6283 closes the circle. PFP verification live.
PHASE 2 AFTER MINT

Decentralized Growth

Expand the Arclings ecosystem and builder tools.

  • Contract-to-API Spec: documentation to query Arclings data directly on-chain.
  • Open-source tools: publish the web-customizer rendering engine code.
  • Developer integration: support independent games and custom visualizers.
PHASE 3 AFTER MINT

On-Chain Synthesis

The final mathematical evolution: Combining the Arcs.

  • Radian Union Engine: An innovative on-chain mechanism allowing holders to merge two open Arclings.
  • Merge & Close: Combine radians to synthesize a new Closed-Circle Grail when sum >= 6.283.
  • Hybrid Visual Traits: Blended patterns and dither schemes from both parent tokens.
  • Deflationary Mechanics: Permanent burning reduces total supply while increasing rarity.
