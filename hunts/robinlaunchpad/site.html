// WHY ROBINLAUNCHPAD

A launchpad that lives
entirely on-chain.

PERMANENCE

Calldata inscriptions

Every asset is written straight into transaction calldata — ordinal-numbered, permanent, and content-deduped. No off-chain host to rot, no pinning service to pay.

SETTLEMENT

Escrow marketplace

Atomic one-transaction listings under the ESIP-2 escrow standard. List, buy, and settle in a single confirmed call.

PRICE DISCOVERY

Timed auctions

Anti-sniping time extensions and enforced creator royalties, on-chain.

SOVEREIGNTY

Non-custodial

The protocol never holds your keys. Assets move only when you sign — custody stays with you, end to end.

INFRASTRUCTURE

Robinhood Chain

Built on the Robinhood Arbitrum L2 — native ETH gas, fast finality, chainId 4663.

STORAGE

On-chain metadata

Traits and media resolve from data: URIs embedded in the chain itself. No IPFS, no gateways, no dead links.
